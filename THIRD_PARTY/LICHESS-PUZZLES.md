# Lichess puzzle database (tactics puzzles)

The Tactics section of Pawnado uses puzzles selected from the **Lichess open puzzle database**
(<https://database.lichess.org/#puzzles>, `lichess_db_puzzle.csv.zst`, downloaded 2026-10-29).

- **Licence:** [Creative Commons CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain dedication). No attribution is required, but credit is given here and in the app: *"Puzzles: Lichess open database (CC0)"*.
- **What we kept:** for every opening the app teaches we picked puzzles whose Lichess opening tag matches that opening (`tools/extract-puzzles.py`): **up to 30 per gambit** and 20 for the other openings, all from the opening phase (the puzzle starts before move 21, most before move 13), rated 450-1700 with a strong preference for fork, pin, skewer, mate-in-1/2, hanging-piece, trapped-piece and back-rank themes, spread over the rating range. Lichess has no opening tag for the Traxler Counterattack and the Fajarowicz, so for those two the script matches the position (Two Knights with a knight on g5/f7 and a black bishop on c5/f2; Budapest with a black knight on e4). Only the puzzle id, start position (FEN), moves, rating and theme names are stored (`tactics/tactics.json`). Every move is re-checked for legality with chess.js (`tools/build-tactics.mjs`).
- **Traps** (ids starting `T-`) are original to this project and are not taken from the Lichess data: a handful of famous traps written out by hand (`tools/traps-src.mjs`), plus traps found by running Stockfish over our own book lines (`tools/mine-traps.mjs` and `tools/curate-traps.mjs` write `tools/traps-mined.mjs`): positions where a natural-looking opponent move loses to a unique refutation. Every line is checked for legality and checkmate with chess.js and re-checked move by move with Stockfish (`ENGINE=1 node tools/build-tactics.mjs`).
- Puzzle ids (`id` column in the database) link back to the puzzle on Lichess: `https://lichess.org/training/<id>`.
- Lichess itself is free software (AGPL-3.0); we use none of its code from this dataset, only the CC0 data.
